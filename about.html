About PriJiva

Spreading civic-sense awareness to make the world “Civically Abled.”

We are a youth-led movement empowering students and changemakers to turn civic knowledge into everyday street-level action.

How PriJiva Began

PriJiva was founded in 2024 by student changemakers who grew tired of everyday civic hurdles—blocked footpaths, reckless driving, unsegregated waste, and missing zebra crossings.

Instead of waiting for others to solve these challenges, they mobilized peers to lead from the front with creativity, respect, and constructive on-ground optimism.

Starting with small weekend pedestrian drives, PriJiva has grown into an active youth network coordinating campus workshops, community cleanups, and local civic audits.

What PriJiva Means

🌍

Pri — Prithvi (Earth)

Our shared planet, neighborhoods, roads, and public spaces.

🌱

Jiva — Life

Every citizen, commuter, pedestrian, and community living together.

PriJiva brings together Prithvi—Earth—and Jiva—Life. It reflects our belief that caring for the places we live in is inseparable from caring for life itself. Through youth-led civic action, PriJiva works toward communities that are more responsible, aware, and Civically Abled.

Our Civic Model

A 4-step cyclical action model turning local awareness into lasting community habits.

01

Identify Blindspots

Mapping neighborhood issues such as unmarked crossings, honking hotspots, and waste piles.

02

Mobilize Youth

Bringing students together with campaign materials, safety guidelines, and clear action goals.

03

Community Action

Executing friendly, high-visibility on-ground awareness drives and street interactions.

04

Policy Dialogue

Sharing civic audit data with municipal authorities and resident associations to secure lasting fixes.
