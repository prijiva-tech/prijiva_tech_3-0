Who We Are

Civic Sense as an Essential Ability

We treat civic sense as a life skill that can be practiced and celebrated. We empower youth to take ownership of shared public spaces through simple, high-energy interventions.

Grassroots Awareness

Bringing civic awareness alive through street theatre, safe crossing campaigns, and friendly driver engagement.

Campus Action Hubs

Supporting student-led civic chapters in schools and colleges to mentor peers and audit local infrastructure.

Audits & Data

Gathering community data on noise, broken walkways, and missing dustbins to present constructive reports to local bodies.
